UNCND

United Nations Commission on Narcotic Drugs

The United Nations Commission on Narcotic Drugs is the main policy-making body for international drug control, working at the intersection of law, health, and enforcement.

Feature

Three Atoms Ahead.. I Am Always Three Atoms Ahead

From the 'Cocaine Bear' of 1985 to today's server-based chemistry, international drug scheduling cannot outrun a local chemist. Why the CND must adopt rapid Early Warning Advisory systems.

"In the age of artificial drugs, yesterday's solution is bound to be tomorrow's loophole."

By Sarah Asraf · 7 min read

Context at a glance
Operation Toofan — Kerala's crackdown yielded 10,000 cases in 100 days, but remains fundamentally reactive.
Fentanyl Supply Chain — $3M worth of fentanyl ingredients can be legally purchased for under $3,600.
Vienna Consensus — Faltering as the CND struggles to balance regulatory speed with state sovereignty.

Committee 05 of 05

WHO

World Health Organization — School Committee

The World Health Organization is the specialized agency for directing international public health and responding to global medical crises.

Agenda Counterfeit Pharmaceuticals and Patent Laws: Eradicating the Global Black Market of Fake Medicines
Op-ed

ΣΠΆΝΙΣ (SCARCITY): WHAT FILLS THE VOID?

When legitimate medicine remains inaccessible or unaffordable, counterfeits fill the gap. Strict enforcement isn't enough if the system itself is fundamentally broken.

"The most difficult issue faced is not how to keep falsified medicines out of the system but to make the legitimate system strong enough."

By Ishita Sudhir · 7 min read

Key Frameworks
TRIPS Agreement — Situated IP protection within broader social and economic welfare.
Doha Declaration — Affirmed the right to use TRIPS flexibilities to protect public health.
MEDICRIME Convention — Treats falsified medical products as criminal conduct requiring cooperation.
